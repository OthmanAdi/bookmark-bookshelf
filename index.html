How reading works For developers Source

Give your saved links a spine.

Bookmark Bookshelf is an open-source 3D bookmark manager. The pages you meant to read stand as volumes on a shelf: drag the row, pull one out, and read the copy you saved with it. Your library stays in your browser and nothing leaves it.

Read what you saved

When you save a link, paste the text you want to read with it. The reader shows that text as a clean page and remembers where you stopped.

  • A link saved with text opens as a page and resumes at the paragraph where you left it.
  • A link saved on its own offers a field to paste the text later and a button to open the original.
  • Your library stays in this browser. The app shows the text you gave it and does not load pages from your links.

Four parts, each usable alone

Shelf
Draws volumes for a pose and reports what was picked. No clock, no gestures, no storage.
Motion
Springs, drag, flick and snap on Pointer Events. It has no idea it is moving books.
Store
A versioned library in local storage with validation, migration, import and export.
Reader
Renders a saved copy as text nodes only, tracks the position and hands it back.

Smaller combinations that run on their own: shelf and motion, reader, store.

Built to be rebuilt

The repository carries a reusable skill for coding agents. Point an agent at it and ask for the whole shelf or for one part: it gets the contracts, the constraints that keep the 3D row accessible, and the checks that prove the result.
